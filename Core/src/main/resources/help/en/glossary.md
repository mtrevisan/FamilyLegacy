# Glossary

The words of Family Legacy that are not everyday language, in alphabetical order, each with the page that explains it in full. If you meet a word in a list or in a menu and it means nothing to you, look for it here.

**Assertion.** One statement of one source, recorded as it is: a birth date, a sex, a parent. Two sources may make two different assertions about the same thing; both stay in the file. See [Research](research.md).

**Attribute.** A characteristic or status of a person or group that is not an event: an occupation, a residence, a title, a religion. The **sex** of a person is an attribute too. See [People and groups](editors-people.md#individual-attribute).

**Audit.** The creation date and the list of changes of a record, with a comment each. The application adds an update by itself when you confirm a change. See [The record editors](editors.md#audit).

**Citation.** The link between a record (or one of its fields) and a source, with *where* in the source (the locator) and *how it relates* (the stance: *supports* or *contradicts*). The source says what the document is; the citation says what you took from it, and for what. See [Source citation](editors-sources.md#source-citation).

**Conclusion.** What *you* decide when assertions disagree: which one is preferred, how well proven it is (*conflicting evidence*, *supported*, *proven*, *disproven*) and why, in the narrative. It never changes or deletes the assertions it judges. See [Conclusion](editors-research.md#conclusion).

**Confidence.** Your current estimate (*low*, *medium*, *high*) that an assertion is true. It can change as evidence is added. See [The record editors](editors.md#confidence).

**Context impact.** A note that says how a historic event or a cultural norm may have acted on a record (a famine that pushed a family to emigrate, a law that forbade a marriage). It is interpretation, not proof that the target took part. See [Context impact](editors-events-places.md#context-impact).

**Cultural norm.** A custom, law or practice of a time and place (an age of majority, a naming convention) that helps to read records or to estimate missing data. See [Cultural norm](editors-events-places.md#cultural-norm).

**Dossier.** The sidebar on the right: everything recorded about the selected individual or group. See [The tree, the graph and the ego network](views.md#the-sidebar).

**Event.** Something that happened, with a type, a date and a place: a birth, a marriage, a burial. It is a record of its own, shared by everyone who took part; a date of birth belongs to the birth *event*, not to the person. See [Events and dates](events-and-dates.md).

**Evidence qualifiers.** Three optional judgements on a citation: whether the source is *original* or *derived*, whether the informant was *primary* or *secondary*, and whether the evidence is *direct*, *indirect* or *negative*. See [Evidence qualifiers](editors-sources.md#evidence-qualifiers).

**Extract.** The passage of a source that you quote or point to (a transcription, a translation, a summary, or a region of a scan), kept once and cited as many times as needed. See [Extract](editors-sources.md#extract).

**FLEF.** *Family LEgacy Format*, the file format of the application (`.flef`). It keeps records and the links between them, not a person with fields inside. See [Getting started](getting-started.md#how-the-data-is-organised).

**Group.** A set of individuals (a family, a household, a congregation) with roles; a group can be part of a larger one. See [Editing people, groups and places](editing.md#groups).

**Historic event.** A broad event that shaped many lives (a war, an epidemic), as opposed to the event of one person. See [Historic event](editors-events-places.md#historic-event).

**Identity hypothesis.** The possibility, kept on file without merging yet, that two records describe the same real person (or group, or place). Accepting it means merging the records. See [Identity hypothesis](editors-research.md#identity-hypothesis) and [Merging](merging.md).

**Individual.** A person. It holds names, attributes and links; dates and places come from the events the person took part in. See [People and groups](editors-people.md).

**Locator.** The part of a citation that says where to look inside the source: volume, page, entry. The [Source Citation Assistant](citation-assistant.md) can build it.

**Participation.** The link between an individual (or group, or place) and an event, with a **role** (*principal*, *witness*, *officiant*, *informant*...). To say that Giovanni was born in an event, he takes part in it as *principal*. See [Event participation](editors-events-places.md#event-participation).

**Privacy level.** *public*, *restricted* or *confidential*, set on a record and applied by the views, the reports and the exports according to the policy chosen. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

**Proof status.** How well an issue is established, in a conclusion: *conflicting evidence*, *supported*, *proven* (the Genealogical Proof Standard is met) or *disproven*. See [Conclusion](editors-research.md#conclusion).

**Record.** Anything with an identifier in the file: an individual, an event, a source, a note... Every record has its own editor. See [The record editors](editors.md).

**Relationship.** A link between two records (the *subject* and the *target*) with a type: parent and child, spouses, group member, associate. For parents, the subject is the *child* and the target is the parent. See [Relationships](relationships.md).

**Repository.** The place that keeps a source: an archive, a library, a parish office. See [Repository](editors-sources.md#repository).

**Research question, activity, task.** A question you want to answer; a piece of work done or planned to answer it (what was searched, and what was found, even nothing); a to-do item that follows. See [Research records](editors-research.md).

**Root.** The person or group the current view is built around. **Set as Root** changes it. See [Navigating the tree](navigating.md#moving-around).

**Source.** A document or other thing that says something: a register, a census, a letter, a photograph. It is not a fact; what you take from it goes in a citation. A source that is a copy or a transcription lists the original it was made from in *Derived from*. See [Sources and media](sources-and-media.md).

**Stance.** In a citation, whether the source *supports* or *contradicts* the assertion it is attached to.

**Story.** A note of kind *story*: narrative prose meant to be read and published (a biography, a family tradition), kept apart from the technical notes of the research. See [The record editors](editors.md#notes).

**Tag.** A free word of your own that classifies a record (*Brick Wall*, *To Verify*). Tags are not in the file: see [The record editors](editors.md#tags).

**Validity range.** *Valid From* and *Valid To*: when a name, an attribute or a relationship held. See [The record editors](editors.md#validity-range).
